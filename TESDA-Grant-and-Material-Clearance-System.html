STVET Administrative Office

Manages scholar grant allocations (TWSP, STEP, PESFA, UAQTEA), calculates training allowances, issues LandBank check vouchers, and prints official clearance certificates.

  • Automated LandBank Check Generation
  • Greenprints Clearance Override Locks

Greenprints Bookstore Partner

Logs required TVET textbook, uniform, and technical tool kit payables for scholars. Issues Official Receipts (OR) upon cash/voucher payment to automatically lift clearance holds.

  • Material Catalog & Item Code Pricing
  • Official Receipt (OR-GP) Logging
  • Real-Time Clearance Status Sync

COA & Regional Audit Monitoring

Provides Commission on Audit (COA) inspectors with immutable audit trails of every check issuance, clearance state change, administrative override, and liquidation report.

Clearance & Disbursement Lifecycle

How the STVET–Greenprints Workflow Operates

Four synchronized steps ensuring zero uncollected liabilities before scholarship check release.

1

Scholar Enrollment

STVET Admin enrolls scholar, specifies TVET qualification course, and computes allowance allocation.

2

Material Payable Logging

Greenprints Bookstore assigns required textbooks, uniforms, or toolkits. Clearance automatically switches to ON_HOLD.

3

Settlement & Receipt

Scholar settles payables via cash/voucher. Bookstore issues OR-GP receipt, immediately changing status to CLEARED.

4

LandBank Check Release

STVET Admin verifies clearance status and safely hands over the LandBank allowance check to the cleared scholar.
