Integrated System Modules & Stakeholder Workflows

How the STVET Admin, Greenprints, and Scholar Student roles collaborate.

STVET Admin

Manages scholar grant allocations (TWSP, STEP, PESFA, UAQTEA), calculates training allowances, issues LandBank check vouchers, and prints official clearance certificates.

  • Automated LandBank Check Generation
  • Greenprints Clearance Override Locks
  • Printable Official Clearance Documents

Greenprints

Logs required TVET textbook, uniform, and technical tool kit payables for scholars. Issues Official Receipts (OR) upon cash/voucher payment to automatically lift clearance holds.

  • Material Catalog & Item Code Pricing
  • Official Receipt (OR-GP) Logging
  • Real-Time Clearance Status Sync

Scholar Student

Lets enrolled scholars check their own clearance status, view outstanding Greenprints material payables, and confirm when their LandBank allowance check is ready for release.

  • Personal Clearance Status Lookup
  • Outstanding Material Payables View
  • Allowance Check Release Notice
Clearance & Disbursement Lifecycle

How the STVET–Greenprints Workflow Operates

Four synchronized steps ensuring zero uncollected liabilities before scholarship check release.

1

Scholar Enrollment

STVET Admin enrolls scholar, specifies TVET qualification course, and computes allowance allocation.

2

Material Payable Logging

Greenprints assigns required textbooks, uniforms, or toolkits. Clearance automatically switches to ON_HOLD.

3

Settlement & Receipt

Scholar Student settles payables via cash/voucher. Greenprints issues OR-GP receipt, immediately changing status to CLEARED.

4

LandBank Check Release

STVET Admin verifies clearance status and safely hands over the LandBank allowance check to the cleared Scholar Student.
